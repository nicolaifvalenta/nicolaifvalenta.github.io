Papers are for settled thoughts. Most of my thoughts are not settled, and I would rather have somewhere to put them than let them evaporate in the shower.

The rule I am trying to follow is simple: write as I would talk to a friend who knows the subject, but keep the standards of someone who would be embarrassed by a sloppy argument.[^1]
 
[^1]: The two halves of this rule disagree more often than you would expect.
